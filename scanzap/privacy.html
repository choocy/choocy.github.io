ScanZap for iPhone and iPad

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

ScanZap helps you scan, import, organize, edit, search, and export documents. This policy describes how the iOS and iPadOS app handles your information. It does not describe unrelated websites or other apps.

Documents and on-device processing

Scans, imported photos and PDFs, OCR text, folders, tags, signatures, and exported files are stored on your device unless you choose a cloud or sharing feature. Scanning, OCR, image enhancement, searching, and editing are performed on the device. ScanZap does not upload document contents to our own servers for these tasks.

The app may request access to the camera, selected photos, or files when you use the corresponding feature. You control these permissions in your device settings. Imported material and any text recognized in it may contain personal or sensitive information you choose to process.

Optional iCloud features

ScanZap Pro can use your Apple iCloud account to back up and sync your editable library across your Apple devices. This can include documents and pages, OCR text, folders, tags, signatures, and related library state. If iCloud is unavailable, the app may continue with local storage. Apple's iCloud service and your Apple Account settings govern that storage and transfer.

A separate Pro feature can copy exported PDFs and JPEGs from Extracted Files into a folder you select in iCloud Drive, manually or through auto-upload if you enable it. These are copies: removing an item inside ScanZap does not automatically remove an earlier iCloud Drive copy. You can turn off auto-upload or remove the destination in the app's cloud settings, and manage iCloud files in Apple's Files app.

Purchases

Optional ScanZap Pro subscriptions are handled by Apple through StoreKit. Apple processes payment and maintains your purchase history under its own policies. The app receives subscription entitlement and transaction status needed to unlock Pro and restore purchases; we do not receive your payment-card details through the app.

Sharing and third parties

When you export or share a document using the system share sheet, a selected destination, or iCloud Drive, the copy goes to the service or person you choose. Their handling of that copy is governed by their own policies. ScanZap does not include an advertising or third-party analytics SDK in the current app build and does not sell your documents or use them for ad targeting.

Retention and your choices

Your local library remains on your device until you remove items, clear app data, or uninstall the app, subject to your device backups and the app's trash/recovery behavior. Synced data and export copies may remain in your iCloud account until removed there. You can manage camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings, manage iCloud in your Apple Account and ScanZap settings, and manage subscriptions in your Apple Account. Uninstalling the app does not cancel an active subscription or necessarily delete iCloud copies.

Security and limitations

Device security and Apple's platform protections help protect app data. Optional locks, hidden folders, and password-protected PDF exports add controls, but no method of storage or transfer is risk-free. Review a document and its destination before sharing, especially if it contains sensitive information.

Children, changes, and contact

ScanZap is a general-purpose document utility and is not directed specifically to children. We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on ScanZap's App Store product page.

For the terms governing app use and subscriptions, see the Terms of Use.
